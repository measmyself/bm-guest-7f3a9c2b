Cinnamon — Homeward Bound

Monday, November 23, 2026 · Set Up For Success
We built Cinnamon's days around one idea: that being unattended in the house is something she earns by fully pottying outside first. She holds her bladder well and keeps her crate clean, so the plan below is built on her strongest habit rather than on fixing a weak one.
THE RULE · FREEDOM IS EARNED
✓
She pees and poops on the walk — she's free, with you, in the room you're in.
Cinnamon sometimes pees without pooping. A pee-only outing earns credit, but it doesn't mean she's finished—allow supervised freedom, and keep watching for the second elimination.
✗
She doesn't — back in the crate, or she stays on watch until the next try.
The crate does not have to be the automatic response to every unsuccessful walk. You have the option of keeping her directly supervised, or in a small, secured area, until the next attempt.

Where habits are most reliable
The one thing we want to avoid as much as possible is leaving her unattended.
Crated is fine. In the room with you is fine. Loose in an empty room is not.
Food and Water
Hand-feed as much as you can — so she stays engaged with you, instead of free-feeding.
During her stay we consistently offered smaller portions than she had been receiving at home, supplemented with fresh ingredients. Her energy, behaviour and stool quality remained stable. We recommend continuing with consistent, measured meals rather than leaving food available through the day.
Water: Offer it regularly through the day rather than leaving the bowl down. Same principle as the meals — it's something she gets from you, at a time you choose.
What's Normal
A pee-only outing. It happens, and it isn't a failure. She's earned the supervised freedom; you just keep an eye out for the rest.
Settling slowly in the first few days. She's moving from our routine to yours. Consistency closes that faster than leniency does.
Asking to come out of the crate. The crate is where her habits are most reliable, and that doesn't change because she'd rather be out.
